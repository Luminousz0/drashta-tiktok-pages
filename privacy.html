Privacy Policy — Drashta Pipeline

Last updated: August 2026

Drashta Pipeline is a personal automation tool used to schedule and post content to a single TikTok account (@drashta.official) that the developer owns and operates.

This app does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data belonging to any third party. It does not process data from any TikTok user other than the developer's own account, used solely to upload video content the developer created.

Video files and associated metadata (captions, hashtags) are processed locally on the developer's own machine and sent directly to TikTok's API for the sole purpose of posting to drafts. No data is retained by any third-party service.
